I am Tian Yixian (田一贤), a first-year master's student in Computer Science and Technology at the College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Fudan University, concurrently enrolled at the Shanghai Innovation Institute. I received my B.E. in Internet of Things Engineering from Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications (BUPT), graduating ranked 3/182 (top 1.6%).

My research interests lie in **multimodal large models** and **vision-language models (VLMs)**, with a focus on video-language data construction and large-scale data quality control, as well as LLM agents and table understanding. Through research internships at the MOSS-VL foundation-model team at Fudan University, Zero Lab at Peking University, the Computer Network Information Center of the Chinese Academy of Sciences, and the NESA Lab at Zhejiang University, I have gained end-to-end experience spanning data construction, model training, and edge deployment.
